# Castle escape

A three-room escape inspired by Harker’s exploration of the locked castle in Chapters II–III. Each room fits on one screen; movement, cover and the visible patrol fans stay consistent. The rooms build a short sequence:

1. **The guest wing:** find a brass key, unlock the dividing arch, lift the stair bolt in the west room, and reach the north stair. The upper masonry prevents a straight perimeter shortcut.
2. **The shuttered chapel:** find the iron key for the cross-door, take the winch handle from the far chamber, and pass the upper patrol to the stair. The wall and pillar require separate approaches to the objects and exit.
3. **The gatehouse:** take the last key, use it to release the winch, raise the chain with the handle, and return to the south gate. Opposed upper patrols and two horizontal patrol lanes guard different crossings.

The challenge comes from connected locks, route choices and overlapping patrol coverage. There is no time limit. Alcoves provide safe places to wait for a visible turn; masonry and closed doors block sight as well as movement. Brief exposure fills a warning, which drains out of sight. Gentler patrols move more slowly and allow more exposure. Pause, room interludes and the final screen freeze movement and patrol time. Continue, Retry this room, Restart this room and Replay all rooms are explicit controls. Small-screen overlays scroll when needed.

Only cleared rooms and the gentler setting are saved, under the versioned game-only localStorage key `newreading-dracula-castle-v2`. Reload begins the latest uncleared room again; retry does not replay earlier rooms. Replay all resets this game checkpoint. Invalid stored data is preserved until an explicit fresh replay, and failed storage writes display a warning while in-memory play continues. Reader notes and progress use separate records. The unchanged return module preserves the reader route and registers the reader’s scoped offline copy. There are no accounts, analytics or external runtime dependencies.

The successful escape, three floor plans, key and bolt placements, winch sequence and patrolling shadows are invented and identified as such in From the novel. Every SVG room mark, character, object, gate and icon is newly authored. Paper, ink and oxblood colours follow the reader. No image services, fonts or copied illustration pixels are used. Original code and drawings use the example’s MIT license.

The only quotation is a contiguous exact excerpt from the pinned edition, chap03 / chap03-b030, credited to Jonathan Harker’s journal, Chapter III, 15 May. The optional source view links to [Project Gutenberg 345](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/345). It requires no later plot knowledge. Full edition notices remain in the reader.
