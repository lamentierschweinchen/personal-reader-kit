## Notices and recovery

The application shows the source notice once for an unchanged three-edition collection in that browser, retains immediate access to the complete license, and makes notice/license reachable again through About this work and Contents. The first and final prepared sections carry the original notices and license into exact-edition and bundle exports. Original formats are available from each linked Gutenberg landing page, at no charge. This follows the project’s quiet-reading rule; it does not strip source licensing to obtain a cleaner layout.

[Project Gutenberg’s license](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html), including 1.E.1–1.E.7, governs retaining its name, notice, full license and alternate-format source access. This reader is supplied without charge; no commercial trademark arrangement or claim of Gutenberg endorsement is made. Source text is not licensed by the reader’s MIT license.

## Source record

Tagore’s preface calls Realisation in Action the last paper, while this digital witness’s contents places Beauty and the Infinite after it. That wording is retained as source evidence and recorded quietly in About this work. It is not used as a central interpretive claim.
