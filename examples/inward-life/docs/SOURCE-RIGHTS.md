## Fidelity and uncertainty

The complete decoded non-whitespace body of each saved HTML witness agrees in source order with its prepared text, including the source apparatus and notices. Explicit BR/TEI verse lines, emphasis, small capitals, page markers, quotation roles, links and both title-page images are preserved in the named profile. Julian’s contents table becomes fixed tab-separated source rows; collapsible HTML whitespace, UI section labels and reading typography are documented adaptations. This is not a facsimile, full proofreading, or complete historical-print collation. Gutenberg itself does not guarantee identity with a single printed source.

Tagore’s preface calls Action the last paper although Beauty and the Infinite follow in this witness’s contents. The words are preserved, with the anomaly recorded quietly in the source record. No uncertain word, historical assertion or contributor role is silently repaired. James’s selected psychological framework remains an argument; it is not presented as a neutral judgment over Julian or Tagore. Physical-device selection and accessibility verification remain separate from source conservation and browser tests. This receipt contains no reader notes, device links, credentials or personal intake.
