# The Inward Life

Three complete English digital editions: Julian of Norwich in Grace Warrack’s1901 version, Tagore’s Sādhanā witness dated1916, and James’s Varieties in its1917 impression. Read each independently, or follow questions of experience, love and action through Three doors. Who is speaking? offers a quieter reading of editorial and interpretive mediation.

Canonical reader: [The Inward Life](https://newreading.app/inward-life/).
