# The Open Boat — source and reuse

Stephen Crane, “The Open Boat,” in *The Open Boat and Other Stories* (William Heinemann, London, 1898), printed pp. [3]–39 in the Bancroft Library copy. The complete seven-part story follows the [Project Gutenberg #45524 transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45524) by Dianna Adair, Steven Calwas and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team. The selected [HTML mirror](https://gutenberg.pglaf.org/4/5/5/2/45524/45524-h/45524-h.htm) has SHA-256 `fb7b1269a70365fc9b3ad9eb2ee29ccdca35d455d1163e8aeccd65a073526104`. The [Bancroft scan](https://archive.org/details/openboatandother00cranrich) was used for comparison. The scan’s printed pagination differs from the digital markers, and three compound words differ in hyphenation. The reading copy preserves the digital witness’s wording, typography encoded in the source, and sequence.

The story appeared in *Scribner’s Magazine* in June 1897. Crane died in 1900. Caroline Norton (1808–1877) wrote “Bingen on the Rhine,” which appears as a four-line recollection in the story. The historical text is in the U.S. public domain and falls outside ordinary U.K. and EU life-plus-70 terms. Reuse elsewhere depends on local law.
