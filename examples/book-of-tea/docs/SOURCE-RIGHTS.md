# The Book of Tea: sources and rights

Okakura Kakuzō wrote *The Book of Tea* in English. This edition follows the **1906 G. P. Putnam’s Sons issue, London and New York**, scanned from the University of California Libraries copy `bookoftea00okakrich`. The copyright leaf names **Fox Duffield & Company**; that is distinct from the title-page imprint. The title/imprint, dedication, detailed contents and all seven chapters are included, with ten historical footnotes and the original verse.
