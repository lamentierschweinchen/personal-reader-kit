# Source and rights: The Little Clay Cart

The source edition is Project Gutenberg eBook 21020, a transcription of the 1905 Harvard Oriental Series publication of Arthur W. Ryder’s translation, with Charles Rockwell Lanman’s series note. Gutenberg credits its transcription contributors in the retained source notice. The full Gutenberg notice and terms remain in the edition. The source is established as public domain in the United States; this is not a determination for every jurisdiction. Translator Ryder died in 1938; series editor Lanman died in 1941.

The public `edition/book.json` is the exact prepared edition, SHA-256 `a60b09c9a93e2549c2ea6e8fb506d81c5d19e310b0767959b96e93d687ae5ed1`, from local project commit `7f779bb741a711e361bbbc33f3aef1bc35039987`. It includes the complete play, source notes, original Gutenberg apparatus and original source images. The source HTML, source scan, research files, personal records and review screenshots are not publication inputs. Transcription fidelity was compared to the source HTML; print-witness review was sampled, not a full proofread.

The article data are the exact locally reviewed selectable reflows of the publishers’ CC BY 4.0 HTML: Amartya Sen’s “Law and Ideas of Justice” and Christopher R. Austin’s “The eropolitical compound.” Their copyright notices, publisher links, license and reflow credits appear with each article. Sen’s author portrait was omitted. Publisher interface and duplicated Austin reference entries and heading are omitted. The full text of Zin, Mukherjee and Kaul is not included; journal links retain their source and access limitations.
