# Objects and scholarship

The companion's historical objects are comparisons; they do not establish when the play was composed or what a stage prop looked like.

- *Toy Model of a Cart*, India, 1st century BCE–1st century CE, terracotta, Metropolitan Museum of Art 1991.455.2. The Met marks its three photographs public domain. [Museum record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38074).
- *One from a Pair of Ear Ornaments*, Andhra Pradesh, ca. 1st century BCE–1st century CE, gold, Metropolitan Museum of Art 1981.398.3. The museum marks the photograph public domain. [Museum record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/39676). Photograph SHA-256 `a4d9b5f2d501bc139f39a7c4ed2b8607a5f2e784be7cfd32681defa08cc47467`.
- *Gaming Die*, Gandhara or Afghanistan, 1st–3rd century CE, ivory, Metropolitan Museum of Art 2000.284.19. The museum marks the photograph public domain. [Museum record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38716). Photograph SHA-256 `7391149dac9f3ad7dcb35f3cb636032fe2bec1fe82a2b043012d63e17280f67c`.
- Mathura sandstone panel, 2nd century CE, Museo d’Arte Orientale, Turin, Inp/9. The [museum](https://www.maotorino.it/it/archivio-catalogo/lo-shakara-e-il-vita/) identifies the figures as probably Śakāra and his companion. Monika Zin identifies the figure behind as a jester and questions whether the scene depicts the play. The unmodified photograph by Sailko is [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); [original photograph and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mathura_(Uttar_pradesh),_mrcchakatika,_shakara_e_vita,_II_sec.JPG). SHA-256 `c0021a4bb66ef79d8cf2d4a5672c998469ed398109c8680cff0e9a740156a9e6`.
- An [Archaeological Survey of India report](https://assets.ctfassets.net/l47hkrce4wip/4Q3b4wS4hdaNQYGffR9TWu/bb26633a9b97d7f4535bbe1f8611f9d5/013_Indian_Archaeology_A_Review_1968-69.pdf), printed page 60, records a bronze coin found at Gadhkalika in Ujjain and identified as an issue of Chandragupta II. This is a find record, not evidence of the play's date.
