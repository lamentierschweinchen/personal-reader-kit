# Independent builder trial: Schopenhauer and Hegel

16 October 2026. Starting kit: `6dc8fb146e7e7ec861f4d5b99d21be6f5b696505`. Project: `schopenhauer-hegel`, branch `codex/schopenhauer-hegel`. The chosen concept was supplied in [CONCEPT.md](CONCEPT.md); the builder refined its readings and unresolved interactions directly. No upstream kit edits, other-reader edits, remote, deployment or human pilot are part of this trial.

## Delivered experience

The noticeboard leads into three source-grounded pairs: disagreement, argumentative victory, and the authority of opinion. Both full supplied editions remain available with independent reading places. Facing passages on tablets become switchable halls on phones; each hall retains its own scroll. Readers can keep either passage or an exact two-source connection, inspect surrounding arguments and source notes, and return to their main book. The record drawer distinguishes a sourced biographical fact, Schopenhauer’s own allegation and editorial framing.

Close reading changed the provisional questions. Hegel’s discussion of historical diversity is not simply a claim about two people arguing; his Sophists passage needs the positive role he has already assigned them; his appeal to original sources includes a qualification. The three orientations preserve those distinctions. Saunders’s ironic characterization of the tactics also matters. A sentence-level contest would misrepresent both the texts and the historical evidence.

## What worked unchanged

The no-install Node shell, loopback server, per-book positions, IndexedDB transactions, state conflict rules, exact annotation anchors, draft retention, private bundle checksum/preview/atomic restore, and content-versioned service worker supplied a useful baseline. `site/db.mjs` and `site/bundle.mjs` remain byte-for-byte inherited. Range-offset context positioning handled a short selection near the end of a real 6,057-code-unit paragraph at enlarged type. Original sample writing and its hash were retained unchanged.

The copied guide was useful in practice: distinguish source coverage from proofreading, inspect unsupported EPUB features, retain damaged data, test a stopped origin with a failing network control, and keep literary reading usable without a companion. Its exception for an already-chosen direction avoided forcing three speculative prototypes after the concept was settled. The intentional focus gestures and deliberate reveal controls could be reused; this project also keeps the full book title visible in focus, alongside the chapter title.

The complete selected range fit within the dialog viewport below its sticky header in both engines. A two-paragraph selection containing a source-note call and printed page marker retained its exact text and segments across reload.
- Close/Escape did not discard an unfinished note after cancellation. Injected save and deletion failures retained the draft and saved data; later successful operations completed normally. Focus preserved title, selection, continuation and keyboard recall/exit.
- Source footnote/backlink navigation, Greek, verse, four tables and all five decoded source images were checked. Settled screenshots cover noticeboard, main reading/large type, focus continuation, context, paired readings and source features. Representative source HTML and adapted views were visually compared. No global horizontal overflow appeared at the tested widths; fixed verse/tables may scroll locally.
- The actual Settings bundle UI exported both full editions, one retained note and three connections. A clean context at a different origin restored them with the source-library request deliberately failing and service workers blocked. - Thirty Node tests cover inherited exact-text, state, bundle, EPUB-rejection, history and publication behavior plus the real editions, dangerous/dangling extension fields and preserved source peculiarities. The build validates the exact reviewed edition bytes, source identity, image inventory and session anchors.

## What this does not establish

This is builder evidence, not newcomer acceptance or proof of learning. No human participant has explained the philosophical differences after using the companion. No physical iPhone/iPad selection, screen-reader journey, e-ink transfer, complete print collation or independent German-text verification was performed. The current actual-book driver does not rerun every inherited multi-tab conflict, corrupt-state repair, overlap-edit or damaged-book browser scenario; the unchanged storage code and Node regressions do not substitute for those missing device/UI checks.

The books are source-catalogue public domain in the USA, not cleared for every jurisdiction. Hegel’s supplied publisher’s device has no separately established worldwide provenance. No destination or audience change has been approved. Images, full text and apparatus are accounted for explicitly without calling the local audit permission to publish.

The source EPUB links are useful for separate e-reader use, but this is not an EPUB exporter. A faithful future exporter needs source notes/backlinks, Greek, lineation/indentation, source pages, contents tables with spans/relative widths, embedded images and volume/translator metadata; it needs EPUB validation and actual reading-system review. It must make companion prose and private annotations opt-in and cannot promise synchronization.

Re-running the pinned adapter into a new private directory reproduced both reviewed edition JSON files byte-for-byte. The adapter does not overwrite the reviewed assets or automatically refresh their inventory.

## Reusable proposals for the kit

1. Return a fuller unsupported-feature/resource inventory from the bounded EPUB inspection, even when candidate creation stops early. Use original fixtures containing cover wrappers, CSS, images, source links and tables.
2. Design a versioned capability profile for source-aware editions and portable bundles. Validate unsupported capabilities explicitly, then add one well-tested representation at a time. Avoid making this book-specific HTML adapter a universal importer.
3. Separate reusable sample provenance from a project’s reviewed publication inventory through a documented extension point. Retain exact bytes, explicit file sets and all-ref history checks; add decoded-media verification as a distinct review.
4. Parameterize real-book acceptance journeys with edition IDs, a long paragraph, a multi-block selection, note/backlink, illustration, verse and cross-work pair. Keep the kit’s fixtures original; send the test pattern upstream without these texts, source captures or private records.
5. Preserve two-work permissions, two reading places and per-pane scroll as independent concepts. Pairing is a temporary reading action, not proof that the reader has completed both chapters.
6. Run the existing bounded human pilot on this finished example: ask a newcomer to open a full book, keep/restore a passage, and explain one difference between the two arguments. Record friction and learning separately from automated success.

These are proposals and a working local example, not upstream changes. The project remains ready for direct review without requiring another speculative design round.
