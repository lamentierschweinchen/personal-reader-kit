## Hegel

[Catalogue](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/51635) · [exact HTML source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/51635/pg51635-images.html) · [source-edition EPUB](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/51635.epub3.images).

The actual title pages identify *Hegel’s Lectures on the History of Philosophy*, volume one, translated by **E. S. Haldane** (Elizabeth Sanderson Haldane). They record first publication in England in **1892**, by Kegan Paul, Trench, Trübner & Co., and a **1955 lithographic reprint** by Routledge & Kegan Paul, printed by Jarrold and Sons. The digitization is of that supplied reprint; it is not described here as an 1892 facsimile.

Haldane’s note says the translation uses the second, amended German edition of **1840**, prepared by **Michelet** from multiple lecture courses and notes. The contents include Haldane’s note, the contents tables, the inaugural address, a prefatory note, the introduction, the source’s “Oriental Philosophy” discussion, Greek philosophy through the later Cynics, **173 footnotes**, and the transcriber’s notes. **Volume 1 of 3 is complete here; the three-volume work is not.**

The catalogue and actual HTML/EPUB rights metadata state US public-domain status. This review covers the supplied Hegel text, Haldane’s translation and notes, Michelet’s editorial material as translated, and the digitized apparatus. Credits: **Giovanni Fini, Fritz Ohrenschall and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team**. The author, translator and contributors are acknowledged in the reader’s source record.

Five images are retained, unchanged and embedded in the prepared edition: the transcriber-created cover, the title-page publisher’s device, and three diagrams accompanying Zeno’s fourth proof (printed p. 276). The source transcriber explicitly places the cover in the public domain. The other four images are part of the catalogue’s US-public-domain volume; no separate illustrator credit is supplied. The publisher’s device is reproduced as source content, without an endorsement claim. The catalogue does not independently establish the origin date or worldwide status of that design. A destination/territory review must consider that contribution before public distribution. Exact image digests and byte lengths are in [the inventory](../editions/inventory.json).

## Schopenhauer

[Catalogue](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10731) · [exact HTML source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/10731/pg10731-images.html) · [source-edition EPUB](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10731.epub3.images).

The title identifies **Arthur Schopenhauer**, translated by **T. Bailey Saunders** (Thomas Bailey Saunders). The translator’s preface is dated **February 1896**. The supplied HTML does not identify a publisher or print impression. Do not infer those details from the catalogue’s generated summary.

The complete supplied digital collection contains the translator’s preface, *The Art of Controversy* (preliminary discussion, basis of dialectic and stratagems), *On the Comparative Place of Interest and Beauty in Works of Art*, *Psychological Observations*, *On the Wisdom of Life: Aphorisms*, and *Genius and Virtue*. Saunders identifies posthumous papers edited by **Julius Frauenstädt** and **Eduard Grisebach**. He explicitly says he omitted some passages from the last three essays and changed some titles. Those decisions are part of this selected edition, and are disclosed in the reader. **Nothing further was abridged by this adapter; this is not an unabridged edition of Schopenhauer’s German writings.**

The catalogue and actual HTML/EPUB metadata state US public-domain status for this supplied edition, including the Saunders translation and preface. E-text credits: **Juliet Sutherland, Josephine Paolucci and the Project Gutenberg Online Distributed Proofreading Team**. The HTML literary body contains no illustrations. The EPUB has an automatically generated PNG cover and an SVG wrapper; neither is part of the selected HTML baseline or copied into this adaptation. Its complete source EPUB remains linked, with its original packaging.

## Source terms and transformation

The [permissions guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) and [license guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) were read alongside the actual source notices. Originals remain byte-for-byte in ignored private storage. This adaptation takes the **acknowledged-source route**: distributor header/footer and full distribution boilerplate are excluded; the name is used only in bibliographical acknowledgements and reference links. It is not used as the reader’s title, branding, promotional endorsement, or a source-book cover added by us. All literary text, author/translator material, printed source apparatus and transcriber’s notes remain. The full original formats, credits and original terms are one link away in each work’s source record. No fee is charged by this local example.

If future publication instead associates the distributor’s trademark with the work outside source acknowledgements, review the complete license requirements, including prominent notice and full-license inclusion. An inventory check is not a legal or territory decision. No blanket declaration of worldwide clearance is made for text, translation, notes or images.
