English translations

John L. O’Sullivan: The Djinns, in Henry Llewellyn Williams’s Selections from the Poetical Works of Victor Hugo, Thomas Y. Crowell & Co., undated, pp.84–87. The catalog’s circa 1887 date is uncertain. Print witness.

Stuart Merrill: six Bertrand pieces, Pastels in Prose, Harper & Brothers, 1890, pp.7–10, 15–18, 21–22, 28–29. Illustrated by Henry W. McVickar. Print witness.

Andrew Lang: The Grave and the Rose, Grass of Parnassus, New Edition, Longmans, Green, & Co., 1892, p.109. Print witness. All sixteen lines are retained; Hugo’s French has twelve.

The historical English contributions have expired U.S. publication terms. Edition-specific evidence does not guarantee reuse in every territory. Original wording and print defects remain; no translation has been revised to agree with its counterpart.

The two windows

Bertrand’s final paragraph of La Ronde sous la cloche is compared with the complete Les Fenêtres by Charles Baudelaire, Petits Poèmes en prose, Michel Lévy Frères, 1869, pp.109–110. Wikisource transcription and contributor history, adapted paragraph markup under CC BY-SA 4.0. The English is Stuart Merrill’s complete translation, Pastels in Prose, 1890, pp.174–175.

Baudelaire’s dedication names Bertrand’s book as an influence. Pairing these windows is an editorial comparison, not a claim of direct derivation.
