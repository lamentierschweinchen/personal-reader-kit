# The Yellow Wall-Paper — sources and reuse

## Story and engravings

Charlotte Perkins Gilman, published as Charlotte Perkins Stetson, “The Yellow Wall-Paper,” *The New England Magazine*, January 1892, pp. 647–656. Jo. H. Hatfield engraved the three signed illustrations. The original magazine pages, including the engravings and decorated initial, are courtesy of the [U.S. National Library of Medicine](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/101504878). Cropped and resized images preserve the original sequence; the magazine-page viewer shows the printed placement. The NLM PDF input has SHA-256 `a0ef70d8e92864449a411e0d85ee08318306d098f63f0988fbae6f3d121e92de`.

The reading text adapts the [Literature in Context TEI edition](https://github.com/LiteratureInContext/LiC-data/blob/9c9ccf255390310c481f639c0870bd8890312db1/data/Gilman/gilman-yellow.xml). Transcription, correction, and markup are credited to University of Virginia students and staff and Nial Buford. The pinned publisher’s edition specifies [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This adaptation reorganizes page-spanning paragraphs, retains the original dividers and emphasis, and makes selected corrections against the NLM scan. It excludes the anthology’s modern annotations. The transcription omits a comma after “her” in “And though I always see her” on p. 655. The printed page is available for comparison. The adaptation is CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution and the [full license](../site/cc-by-sa-4.0.txt) accompany portable copies. The publisher’s per-work license is recorded in the pinned XML; the anthology’s web footer carries conflicting generic license labels.

Gilman’s “Why I Wrote the Yellow Wallpaper?” appeared in *The Forerunner*, October 1913. The complete article follows the one-page clipping from the [Schlesinger Library, Radcliffe Institute, Harvard University, via NLM](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/exhibition/theliteratureofprescription/exhibitionAssets/digitalDocsDetail.html). The clipping image is excluded. Its input SHA-256 is `7450db63ccfc284355410094d8eaac2dac06d3259dbf654f2fba8b42ae759250`.

The 1892 and 1913 historical works are in the U.S. public domain. Gilman died in 1935 and Hatfield in 1928, beyond ordinary U.K. and EU life-plus-70 terms. ## Wallpaper study and type

The Metropolitan Museum of Art provides the Open Access public-domain photographs of William Morris’s [*Marigold*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/384018), 1875, 23.163.4c, and [*Pink and Rose*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/337071), about 1890, 23.163.4a. The reader uses resized JPEGs. The study cites Morris’s [“Some Hints on Pattern-Designing”](https://www.marxists.org/archive/morris/works/1881/hints.htm), delivered 1881 and printed 1899, and Rhoda and Agnes Garrett’s [*Suggestions for House Decoration in Painting, Woodwork, and Furniture*](https://archive.org/details/suggestionsforh00garrgoog), Macmillan, 1877 printing, pp. 69–70. The Garretts’ scan is linked; no scan image is packaged.
