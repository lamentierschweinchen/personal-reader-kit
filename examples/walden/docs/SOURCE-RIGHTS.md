## Image provenance and limits

The actual title image shows the title, author, previous work, cabin engraving and Baker-Andrew signature. The [University of Utah rare-books description](https://openbook.lib.utah.edu/tag/walden-pond/) attributes the 1854 title-page engraving to Baker-Andrew after Sophia E. Thoreau's sketch. It attributes the pond plan to Henry David Thoreau, lithographed by S. W. Chandler & Bro. These are historical artwork identifications, not an invented identity for the scanner of Gutenberg's particular files. Gutenberg does not separately name that scanner or provide an image-specific rights certificate. The project relies on the edition's US public-domain declaration and retains this uncertainty for publication review.

[Concord Free Public Library's manuscript survey record](https://concordlibrary.org/special-collections/walden/1) corroborates a manuscript survey dated [1846]. Its online scans have separate stated publication-permission requirements; none of those images is reproduced here. The on-page survey is the Gutenberg edition's printed plan. Its measurements and labels are historical, not current access or route guidance.

Alt descriptions are original editorial accessibility descriptions and do not replace the source pixels. They transcribe prominent labels but are not a complete accessible transcription of every sounding on the map; detailed visual use still requires enlarging the original.

## Publication boundary

`npm run audit:kit` retains the copied scanner unchanged. It rejects binary data, including these two JPEGs, and cannot judge public-domain status or interpret base64 pictures. `npm run audit:public` performs the same complete-history inspection and an additional narrow image review: working file, index, every locally reachable historical version and embedded image must match the separately reviewed original hash. Only the two named image findings can be resolved this way; arbitrary binaries, private paths, credentials and unreviewed history remain failures. A passing project audit is an inventory/provenance receipt, not permission to publish.
