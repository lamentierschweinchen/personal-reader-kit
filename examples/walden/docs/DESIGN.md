# Walden visual direction

A spare field notebook and survey: crisp cool paper, graphite and lake ink, upright labels, open asymmetrical margins and fine ledger rules. The distinction should survive grayscale. Use system sans-serif for authored headings, a small monospaced index for navigation metadata, and the existing book serif for long reading. No downloaded font or new dependency.

Walden and Thoreau come first. On tablet the opening sits beside the original historical pond survey; on phone the reading action precedes the figure. The whole, unfiltered source image remains legible and opens at its original size. It is an edition illustration, not a proposed route or invented geography. The home figure does not disclose later prose, change Companion permissions or replace a saved reading place. Civil Disobedience has its own ruled entry, action and progress, outside the pond composition. Imported books follow those two works.

Normal reading uses a narrow control strip and a measured prose column with an open outer margin. On short landscape screens the masthead scrolls away and only the 53px reading strip persists. Focus retains the source title, text, selection and continuation while hiding chrome until deliberate recall. Paragraphs, verse, emphasis and all edition bytes remain untouched. Fixed accounts keep monospaced columns and keyboard-accessible local scrolling; an authored hint explains the sideways movement. Source figures stay in their original positions in the book as well as the home reference.

Keeping account reads as one sourced essay. Its costs, measurements and tax sections use rules and modest headings, with actual source-passage and bibliography links beside the argument. Reading permissions and the word/comparison tools sit above the essay. The substantial prose follows the reader’s type-size preference; interface labels remain compact. Paper, Dusk and Large print remain independent of the companion.

## Interface icons

Use the shared SVG arrow in authored UI with `currentColor`, `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`. The app icon is an original vector W. Preserve every source punctuation mark. Touch controls retain at least 44×44 CSS pixels, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior and the recoverable focus-mode exit. Source text links retain the book’s own inline layout.

This follows the applicable layout/interaction guidance from the installed frontend-design-guidelines skill, adapted to this existing plain-JavaScript shell. The explicit refinement direction superseded its optional brand-question workflow. No outside-project telemetry or configuration was written. Private settled captures and geometry receipts live in `.reader/reports/refinement/`; the browser suite records phone/tablet portrait and landscape evidence rather than claiming physical-device verification.
