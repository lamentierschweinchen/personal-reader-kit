Reading this edition

The XML headers identify July 2016; the download page’s update dates differ. Current lemma readings, speaker order, editorial brackets, encoded earlier readings, stage directions and songs remain available. The source release is labeled “Text released without textual notes”: print explanatory glosses are not included. Source introductions and the whole-play synopsis remain secondary; the synopsis is labeled for spoilers.

Comma-led speaker cues remain with their speakers. Independent singing and role cues have their own lines. Five independently laid-out embedded cues are separate stage-note blocks; the exact source characters and explicit separators, song emphasis and shared-line insets are retained. Reflow and approximate insets are not a facsimile of print geometry. Structural checks and representative source comparisons do not establish full human proofreading.

The complete source notice travels inside exact-edition downloads and reading bundles. Text sharing includes it too. Personal notes are separate private records. This profile omits quotation cards because the stock card layout does not preserve its full notices and source insets.
