# Learning to See

Status: selected public reader; source-project design direction, 18 October 2026.

Visual thesis: a precise drawing manual on warm paper, with charcoal geometry and one muted brick accent; the book's own marks carry the visual weight.

Two structural decisions: the entry pairs a large original vector leaf with a ruled two-book list; ordinary reading reserves the full measure for prose and uncrowded plates. Companion studies live in a separate studio with their own source visits and no change to the main reading place.

Typography: self-hosted Alegreya for reading and titles, with real italic and variable weights 400–900; compact system sans for tools. Its calligraphic rhythm suits drawing instruction while leaving the historical plates primary. Palette tokens live in `site/collection.css`. Paper, Dusk and Ink remain available. There is no simulated manuscript texture or attributed historical artwork on the cover. The cover drawing is an original vector composition created with an AI coding assistant, not a Ruskin or Crane drawing.

Content plan: collection title and direct book actions; two complete editions; quiet study entrance. Interaction thesis: source-image enlargement, a reversible comparison reveal, and an immediate tile-to-repeat transform. Reading itself has no decorative motion. All transitions respect reduced motion.
