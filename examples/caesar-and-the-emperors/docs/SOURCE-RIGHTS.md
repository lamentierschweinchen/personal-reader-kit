# Source and rights record

This is the selected public copy approved for `https://newreading.app/rome/` on October 24, 2026, from reviewed source commit `1739dcae5c975ac3297f3f8cbcc7b43f0ac53a88`. The exact prepared files are renamed from `.reader.json` to `edition/*.json` without changing any bytes. `edition-lock.json` and EDITION-INVENTORY.json pin their full data, source identities, embedded assets and runtime. | Pliny and Trajan | Complete modern Book X.33–34 and 37–38, 1905 XLII–XLIII and XLVI–XLVII; Melmoth revised by F. C. T.
