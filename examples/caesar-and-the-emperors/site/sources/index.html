The emperor’s postbag — William Melmoth · revised F. C. T. Bosanquet · George Bell 1905

Pliny’s Letters, translated by William Melmoth (1710–1799), revised by the Rev. F. C. T. Bosanquet (1847–1928), London: George Bell and Sons, 1905. Complete letters XLII–XLIII and XLVI–XLVII, printed pp.364–366; modern Book X numbering 33–34 and 37–38. These are early-second-century exchanges between Pliny and Trajan, not letters by Augustus or Marcus Aurelius. PG2811 served as a draft; the selected prose and all relevant notes were collated to these three scan pages. Print wording, the fire-men spelling, note symbols, Latin italics and pound conversions are retained; no full-volume proofreading is claimed. The printed monetary total is preserved, without modern reconstruction. Exact page facsimiles travel in this edition for reference; they contain adjoining material outside the four selected letters. Historical text is public domain in the United States; copyright status outside the United States has not been reviewed for this edition.
