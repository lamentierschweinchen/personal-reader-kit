Transcription alternatives and gaps

The 1787 scan at printed p.128 reads Heldenſöhne. The TEI instead encodes sic Heldenſöhue and corr Heldenlöhne; neither alternative is silently substituted for the inspected print. This reader follows the visible printed word and preserves the two digital readings here. The TEI’s supplied letter in f[o]rt is bracketed. Its three one-character gaps, at printed pp.27, 39 and 42, appear as [illegible character]; the uncertain marks in the scan are not guessed. Physical line wraps and source spelling otherwise remain.
