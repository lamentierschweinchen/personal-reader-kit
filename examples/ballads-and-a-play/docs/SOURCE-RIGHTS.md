## Exact witnesses and contributors

- **Erlkönig, German:** the checked [German Wikisource transcription](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Erlk%C3%B6nig), from *Goethes Werke*, vol.1, Gütersloh: Bertelsmann, undated, p.104. No 1815 facsimile is asserted. **The Erl-King, English:** Edgar Alfred Bowring (1826–1911), in *The Poems of Goethe*, Cambridge Edition, edited and revised by F. H. Hedge and Leopold Noa, Boston: S. The 1882 publication provides the US edition basis.
- **Lenore, German:** Karl Reinhard’s 1817 *Gedichte* (Göttingen: Dieterich), pp.68–83, [Wikisource revision 4220259](https://de.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=4220259). This is distinct from the facing German pages in the English source. **Leonora, English:** William Robert Spencer (died 1834), London: T. Bensley for J. Edwards and E. & S. Harding, 1796, English pp.3–35; [source PDF](https://www.gottfried-august-buerger-molmerswende.de/spencer_leonora_beauclerc_1796.pdf). Thirty-two complete eight-line stanzas. - **Der Handschuh, German:** own transcription of *Gedichte von Friederich Schiller*, Erster Theil, Leipzig: S. L. Crusius, 1800, pp.139–142; [print witness](https://archive.org/details/dlibra.bibliotekaelblaska.pl.92252m.48809386). This is the glove-throwing version, not the first printing’s bowing ending. The difference is discussed after the poem.
- **Der Taucher and Die Kraniche des Ibycus, German:** *Musen-Almanach für 1798*, pp.119–130 and 267–277; checked [Taucher](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Der_Taucher) and [Kraniche](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Die_Kraniche_des_Ibycus) transcriptions. Original spelling, stanza structure and meaningful emphasis are retained.
- **The three Schiller poems, English:** Sir Edward Bulwer-Lytton (1803–1873), *The Poems and Ballads of Schiller*, second edition, Edinburgh and London: William Blackwood and Sons, **1852**; [print witness](https://archive.org/details/poemsballadsofsc00schi). - **Iphigenie auf Tauris, German:** Goethe, *Iphigenie auf Tauris. Ein Schauspiel. Ächte Ausgabe*, Leipzig: Georg Joachim Göschen, **1787**. The [DTA-derived TextGrid/Kolimo TEI](https://textgridlab.org/1.0/aggregator/html/textgrid:4bj9p.0) explicitly marks this file [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). All five acts and twenty scenes remain, with cast and setting. No DTA page image is published here.
- **Iphigenia in Tauris, English:** Anna Swanwick (1813–1899), the undated Arthur Hinds & Co., 4 Cooper Institute, New York printing transcribed in [Project Gutenberg 15850](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/15850).
