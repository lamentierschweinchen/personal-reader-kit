Controls & credits

Hold the arrow buttons, A / D, or the keyboard arrows to steer. Tap a gold sailor, press their number, or press Space to send the most impatient sailor back. Three rock hits wreck the hull; three escaped winds carry the ship away. P or Escape pauses. A new run takes 72 seconds.

Original game, vector drawings and synthesized sound. Colors and type follow the Odyssey reader. Episode reference: Homer, The Odyssey, Book X, Samuel Butler translation; Project Gutenberg ebook 1727. No scores or personal data are stored.

This is a visual action game. Keyboard controls are supported; it does not have an equivalent nonvisual mode.
