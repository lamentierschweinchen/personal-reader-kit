# Source editions and rights

Reviewed for this package on 21 October 2026. The owner approved the complete Nietzsche edition, including its poetry, and Epicurus's complete letter. The source objects are unchanged from the accepted reader. Publication permission for this package does not expand the scope to another work or establish rights in every country.

## The Joyful Wisdom

The witness is T. N. Foulis's 1910 English edition: Nietzsche's text, Thomas Common's prose translation, poetry by Paul V. Cohn and Maude D. Petre, and Oscar Levy's editorial apparatus. [Project Gutenberg 52124](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/52124) names these contributors and classifies the edition as public domain in the USA. The [HTML witness](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/52124/52124-h/52124-h.htm) supplies the literary text; the [canonical digital edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/52124/pg52124-images.html) supplies its complete header and license.

The package preserves all 383 aphorisms, the title and contributor material, editorial note, second-edition preface, *Jest, Ruse and Revenge*, all five books, the appended songs and source notes. Emphasis, source spelling, punctuation, Greek, lineation and stanza spacing remain as prepared. Printed poetry permission notices remain with the edition.

Gutenberg lists no life dates for Cohn. A reported 1956 death date has not been independently established against a primary register. If it identifies this translator, the ordinary [UK life-plus-70 term](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts) raises a question through the end of 2026. The complete poetry is included as expressly accepted; this package claims a US basis, not completed UK or worldwide clearance.

## Letter to Menœceus

This is the complete letter in Book X, chapter XXVII of Diogenes Laërtius, *The Lives and Opinions of Eminent Philosophers*: C. D. Yonge's translation, G. Bell and Sons, London, 1915 reprint. [Project Gutenberg 57342](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/57342) classifies this witness as public domain in the USA and gives Yonge's dates as 1812–1891. The [exact HTML witness](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/57342/57342-h/57342-h.htm) includes the Theognis quotation and source note 142, both retained here. The surrounding *Lives*, other letters and maxims are outside this package's literary scope.

## Digital notices, fidelity and images

Complete Gutenberg headers, licenses, contributor credits and source-volume transcriber notices are embedded in each book's context. They remain available offline under Sources and travel in exact-edition exports and reading bundles. [Gutenberg's permissions policy](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) explains its US basis and the separate conditions attached to its name and digital editions.

These are reflowing reading editions. Page numbers, duplicated contents and decorative images were excluded during preparation; this is not a facsimile or an independently proofread critical edition. The original Nietzsche title-page image and portrait are not packaged. The circle-and-horizon entrance and installation mark are original vector work from the accepted reader. No external image, font binary or typeface license is required; typography uses installed Baskerville/Times and Helvetica Neue/Arial fallbacks. `install-artwork.json` pins the original SVG and three opaque PNG renders.

`edition.json` pins both admitted data files and the canonical identity of each complete book object. Packaging compares those objects against the accepted source revision, including context and notices, not only reading text. The optional comparison is unchanged; it is editorial interpretation with exact source anchors. The immutable Nietzsche object retains an earlier local-publication note for bundle compatibility. A narrowly verified presentation override supplies current rights wording when that exact built-in edition is displayed; imported or altered contexts are not rewritten.
