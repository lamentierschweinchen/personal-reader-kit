## Translation and transcription

The digital witness is [Project Gutenberg eBook 16328](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16328), *Beowulf: An Anglo-Saxon Epic Poem*, translated by J. Lesslie Hall (1856–1928). Gutenberg credits David Starner, Dainis Millers and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team. Gutenberg identifies the work as public domain in the USA; its trademark and redistribution terms remain separate. The entire supplied notice and terms are retained in the recoverable source apparatus.

The comparison witness is the [Library of Congress’s 1892 Boston edition](https://www.loc.gov/item/15005066/), with its collection rights statement. It is not established as the exact printing behind Gutenberg. At I.8 it reads “Waked,” while the declared digital witness reads “Waxed”; the reader preserves the latter. No scan or raw source archive is bundled.

The unchanged prepared edition contains 43 fits, 3,112 poem lines, 132 source notes, 448 marginal summaries, 123 page locations and 555 internal destinations. The source review compared 4,098 source/apparatus items. This is structural and digital-witness fidelity, not full literary proofreading or a claim that historical editorial defects have been repaired. Source headings and glossary can disclose later events.

## Publication boundaries

Only the explicit files in this package enter the public kit and existing `/beowulf/` route. The code license does not relicense source content or imagery. Raw HTML/PDF archives, intake, browser collections, private backups, test screenshots and research are excluded. Other personal readers and Proof remain outside this permission.

The ship-and-mast home-screen icon is an original vector design; the three PNG sizes are its raster exports. No third-party image or source text is contained in those icons.
