Territorial boundary: the cited Gutenberg assertion establishes US status. The catalogue’s intended internet audience extends beyond that jurisdiction; this receipt does not claim worldwide clearance, especially for unidentified translated or quoted contributions. The coordinating editor should retain that limitation in public admission. [Gutenberg’s jurisdiction and edition guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) explicitly distinguishes its digital edition from a particular historical print source. Full [Gutenberg terms](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) are recoverable from Sources & artwork and linked in edition metadata. Exported books preserve attribution and source links; the self-contained candidate also includes the full source notices.

Functional tests, source hashes and a publication scanner do not decide rights or editorial acceptance. The candidate manifest pins each exact file for review and does not authorize an unreviewed directory upload.

## Source terms in downloads and recovery

The original notices in these two source files apply to their Gutenberg-branded distribution. Their sections 1.E.1, 1.E.4–1.E.6 require the usage sentence and access to the full terms, prohibit detaching the terms, and address alternate formats. The official [license](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) and [permissions guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) were checked again on 18 October 2026. A hosted link alone did not make an exported edition self-contained: the previous book/state bundle omitted the full notices. That claim is superseded.

Current collection exports attach the exact full source footer/license strings as a bounded `beside-the-bed/source-notices@1` supplement. Both the full reading bundle and the one-edition download include it. The records bind the notice hash, original source-file hash and canonical hash of the entire unchanged prepared book, including its metadata. Altered terms, a notice for another edition, unknown profiles and mismatched book bytes are rejected. The outer transport checksum also covers the supplement. This is an integrity check against this project's reviewed pins, not a general rights validator or digital signature.

Restore commits the books, reading records and supplement together in existing browser storage. Sources & artwork → Source credits and full Gutenberg terms recovers the full text from that storage without the original source endpoints, including after an offline reload. Credits, source-record links and the complete license strings remain attached; no Gutenberg name or notice is removed. Required publication/first-use presentation must remain in the coordinator's public integration. No repeating chapter gate has been added here.

These are data exports for a compatible Beside the Bed reader, not standalone reading applications. The exact-edition download is a one-book reader bundle without annotations, restored through Settings. A generic older kit reader rejects the project-specific supplement; the current matching reader accepts legacy bundles but warns when their collection notices are missing. New collection-edition exports refuse to download without the checked full notices. Separate passages/progress backups contain records only. Browser storage and application availability still need their own backups. The US-only evidence and unresolved territorial contributions above remain unchanged; transport does not establish additional publication rights.
