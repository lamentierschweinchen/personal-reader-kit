# Beside the Bed

Status: active. Creative direction delegated to the builder and coordinating editor.

Visual thesis: pale linen, deep green ink, and an empty chair crossed by window light; domestic scale, crisp edges, no sepia nostalgia.

Content plan: a quiet cover, two independent books in source order, a separate table of three studies, saved marginalia, and recoverable edition records.

Interaction thesis: ordinary reading stays still; a short entrance and navigation underline orient the reader; study annotations appear only after a deliberate action. Reduced motion removes nonessential motion.

Two system type stacks: Georgia / Iowan Old Style for literature and titles, platform sans for navigation. Original geometric vector art, created for this collection, is an editorial motif and not a depiction of a historical hospital or room.

Paper: #f3f2e9. Ink: #203c36. Muted: #52635d. Accent: #315e50. Rules: #b8c1b6. Dusk retains the same contrast hierarchy. No remote font or image requests.
