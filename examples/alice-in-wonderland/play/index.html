THREE COURSES. STILL SIX O’CLOCK.

An exacting tea party.

Keep the cups filled through three short courses. Hold a cup to pour and release near its dotted line for a bonus. Tap the kettle to refill. Three complaints lose the current course; Retry starts that course again.

In course two, tap a bread-and-butter request before its countdown ends. Serving takes a moment; that guest drinks more slowly while chewing. In course three, a seat change can leave a dirty cup. Tap Clean cup before pouring into it. Replacing it discards the tea, so choose your moment.

The table stops for a reading break before the first seat change, bread request and dirty cup. Read the exchange and instruction, then choose Continue. Tea, requests and every timer wait. Pause reopens the latest exchange. Your cleared courses are remembered in this browser tab. Replay all three starts a new run. Reader notes and reading place are separate.

Keyboard: hold 1, 2 or 3 to pour left, middle or right. Q, W and E serve those seats’ requests. R refills; P or Escape pauses. Tab and held Enter/Space also work on the cup buttons.

Characters and setting from Lewis Carroll’s Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, Chapter VII, “A Mad Tea-party.” Butter in a watch, tea and bread-and-butter, and the Hatter’s demand for a clean cup all appear there. These riddles, service rules and drawings are new adaptations, not Carroll quotations or Rackham illustrations.

Source edition: Carroll, with Arthur Rackham’s illustrations
