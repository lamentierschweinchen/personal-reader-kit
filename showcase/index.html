Add a reader to your home screen

Open a reader, then add it to your home screen.

iPhone & iPad
In Safari, open Share and choose Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if shown.
Android
In Chrome’s menu, choose Install or Add to Home screen. The label varies by version.
Computer
Use Chrome’s Install page as app, or Safari’s File, then Add to Dock.

Open the new icon while online. In Settings, use Check offline copy. Then reopen it with Wi-Fi and mobile data off. Keep a reading bundle as a backup of your books and notes.
