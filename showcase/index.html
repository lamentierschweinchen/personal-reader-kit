What happens to the words?

The original wording, emphasis and structure come first. Any converted book needs checking against the source; new features live around it.

What do I need?

A computer, a coding app, and a book you own or one in the public domain. The kit is free and open source; your AI app may have plan costs or usage limits.

What stays private?

Imported books and notes stay in your browser. This site counts visits and a few actions through Vercel, without collecting your text, searches or reading place. Files you give an AI app may be processed by its provider. Privacy details.

Can I make one for someone else?

Yes, with a public-domain book or one you have permission to share. Owning a book doesn’t give permission to publish its text.
