## Product and editorial changes

Local reading is independent of optional demonstration content. Recovery is an intentional transaction rather than a raw export dead end. Settings exports exact editions and explains device/domain migration and the trust placed in future same-origin code. The file-handoff guide explains the agent/model processing boundary separately from the finished app’s local storage.

Echo index accepts chosen literal text across permitted books. Facing pages accepts kept passages and stores two edition-aware anchors with each connection; legacy unbound reflections remain readable. Focus hides companion administration while retaining title and continuation. Phone comparison chrome is compact; short landscape views keep one reading toolbar sticky.

The workflow delivers ordinary reading first, keeps appearance independent of exploration, and uses three material-led prototypes for an undecided exploratory choice. An already chosen direction is refined without restarting that ceremony. The [Westminster Bridge case](COMPANION-CASE-STUDY.md) records edition disagreement, historical evidence, uncertainty and reasons to decline a mechanism. Its original editorial prose does not distribute the poem, maps or private reader content.

## Remaining limits

The earlier floating focus-exit overlap and subsequent persistent-strip distraction are resolved by hiding controls until deliberate recall. Physical comfort, assistive-technology behavior and platform safe areas still require device review.

Physical iPhone/iPad selection handles, menus, safe areas, orientation, text zoom and storage retention still require actual devices. Browser emulation and DOM Range selection do not establish those behaviors. The four public example deployments and fresh-browser restores are verified above. A first-time reader’s end-to-end GitHub/Vercel onboarding, physical cross-device transfer and an authenticated hosted-book model remain unverified; the stock original-sample root app was not separately deployed in this launch.

The research case is a sourced editorial brief, not an on-site survey, a verified current itinerary, expert-reviewed scholarship or a demonstrated learning outcome. Those checks are explicit next work. The fictional listening walk remains labeled fiction. No automatic research or companion generation is implemented.

The [consolidated coverage table](REVIEW.md#consolidated-handoff-16-october-2026) separates existing fixes, newly implemented behavior, documented guidance and deliberately optional capabilities. Explicit overlap choices, failed extension/removal, substantial companion typography and larger concurrent records are now tested. Native selection gestures, screen-reader use and real reader/editorial feedback remain unverified. There is no standalone bookmark/reset or sync service in this kit.

EPUB/PDF conversion remains assisted. The optional EPUB preparer supports only the documented unstyled text-only subset and creates a provisional candidate requiring source comparison; many ordinary EPUBs need a broader adapter. There is no DRM bypass, image/table renderer, interactive source-footnote engine, linguistic/semantic search, live routing, automatic sync or authenticated hosted-book backend. Preserve exact prepared editions, raw sources and private backups. Existing historical notes recover as quotations when their source is absent or changed; the app does not guess new anchors.
