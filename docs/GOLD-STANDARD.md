# The current reader benchmarks

Reviewed November 9, 2026. These are references for particular strengths, not a template or a blanket endorsement of every surface. Open the relevant experience before commissioning a companion. A later revision must earn its own judgment.

## Playful interpretation: Odyssey — Homeward

[Play Homeward](https://newreading.app/odyssey/play/).

Steer through rock passages while the crew reach for the wind bag. Hull damage and lost knots have different consequences. In Athena’s crossing, the swells pause while the crew need attention. The source gives the game its conflict; the controls let someone experience divided attention and a precarious homecoming. Ship, sea, crew, motion and short crossings belong to one recognizable world.

Examine the relation between a crew warning, the player’s response and the resulting loss. That relationship is the benchmark. Copying the timer, ship artwork or four-stage structure would not transfer it to another book. The game openly rearranges episodes and invents mechanics; it is not a reconstruction of Homer’s voyage or a complete interpretation of the epic.

## Atmosphere and progression: Dracula — Castle escape

[Enter Castle escape](https://newreading.app/dracula/play/).

Harker’s confinement becomes a spatial problem. Leave cover, watch patrols, find tools and open a route through five distinct rooms. In the library, the crowbar and winch handle give the room an intelligible purpose; the floor plan helps someone reason about a place larger than the current view. Architecture, limited sight and carried tools sustain the same imagined escape.

Examine one room’s opening, play it, inspect the plan and return to its journal passage. Mood must survive actual use. Gothic decoration around interchangeable puzzles would miss the achievement. This is an invented escape linked to the novel, and its visual timing requirements are not an accessibility model for every reader.

## A small useful addition: The Book of Tea — Make tea

[Open The Book of Tea](https://newreading.app/book-of-tea/), then **Make tea**.

Leaf quantity, water, temperature, method and an adjustable infusion timer support making an actual cup while reading. The book’s historical discussion remains distinct from the modern brewing guidance. The action needs little explanation and fits the occasion.

Examine whether the method gives enough information to act and whether the tool lets reading continue. This is a benchmark for useful scope, not evidence that every short companion is sufficient. Adding a timer to an unrelated book would add no comparable value.

## A focused reference for curation: The Little Clay Cart

[Open the current companion](https://newreading.app/clay-cart/#companion).

Its Mathura sculpture is accompanied by a museum identification and a scholar’s competing reading. The disagreement changes how one looks at the object. Historical objects and identified scholarship add material beyond the play. Inspect the actual objects and the writing they lead to; the replaced relationship-diagram draft is not this reference.

Use this as a reference for presenting evidence and disagreement. An attractive object gallery or a list of essays alone is not proof of a strong companion. Each selected item still needs to earn its relationship to the work.

## Apply the standard

Choose the closest benchmark for the proposed benefit. Before substantial implementation, use a worked sample: a playable encounter, a complete historical reading with its evidence, an image study with a consequential observation, or a tool that accomplishes its task. State what someone gains beyond ordinary reading and show where it happens. If removing the companion loses only a rearrangement of passages or a few generic observations, reconsider its premise.

Judge literary purpose, substantive contribution, pleasure and craft together. Accuracy and working controls remain prerequisites. Length, restraint, feature counts and another agent’s approval do not establish the result. A richer brief cannot be reduced to a bare reader; a requested simple reader need not acquire a companion.

Use the existing [agent guide](AGENT-GUIDE.md) and [reader-copyeditor](../skills/reader-copyeditor/SKILL.md). Let a reviewer encounter the sample before receiving the maker’s argument for it. A reviewer who wrote the treatment is reviewing their own creative premise; record that relationship and obtain a fresh judgment when independent review is required. The coordinator remains responsible for the final decision.

Keep the ambition and specificity of these references; develop the new book’s own form. Do not reuse their typography, prose rhythm or interaction merely because they passed before.

## Review limits

This selection combines current rendered inspection with the examples’ implementation and existing review records. The November 9 pass sampled live Homeward and Castle escape play, source/plan views, Tea’s method and existing timer-completion state, and Clay Cart’s object and essay presentation. It did not replay every stage, brew tea, retest offline behavior or establish physical-device or human-reader acceptance. It is an editorial benchmark, not a new release certification. Only public examples are included; their sources and publication permissions do not transfer to another edition.
